# Pocket Game Night 1.6.0 (release candidate rc-2026-10-26b)

A new game, **Mafia**: the phone deals secret roles, runs the night as it is passed round, and keeps the score, so nobody has
to sit out as the moderator. Tambola, Impostor, Secret Words and What Am I? play as in 1.5.1.

## For the host
- **Mafia** on "Host a game", for 5 to 16 players on one phone. The app suggests how many Mafia, plus a Doctor and a Police
  from 6 players; change the count within the rules if you like.
- **The deal:** the phone goes round; each player sees their own role privately (hold to see, or tap) and passes it on.
  "Someone saw my role" deals again.
- **The night, passed round:** every player, in or out, takes a turn on an identical screen with the same names, so
  nobody across the table can tell roles from the screen or the number of taps. The Mafia pick, the Doctor protects, the
  Police checks one name (the first tap counts), and the Villagers guess. The phone works out the result.
- **The morning:** a short funny story says what happened and, for a player who went out, their role.
- **The day:** a talking timer, then everyone points at once on "Point!" and the host enters who got the most fingers;
  ties get one more point, then nobody is out.
- **Game over** shows who was who and what happened each night; Play again, End for tonight and a summary of the night.
  History keeps each game.
- Players can join for the next game or leave at any time; a leaver's role stays secret until the game ends.

## Privacy
- No screen the room sees ever shows a role, a night pick or a Police answer before the game ends.
- Every role's night screen looks the same: same names, same tags ("you", "out"), same boxes and sizes; a tap the role
  can't make simply does nothing.
- Ending a game early keeps the roles hidden while Undo is still offered.

## Also in this release
- Version 1.6.0. Saved games from earlier versions still open. Mafia saves its game nights in the same versioned format,
  tied to the morning stories' edition, so a later story list never changes an old night.
- Home's Host line names all five games; Mafia's card comes after What Am I?.

## Testing for this release
- Every rule test (rules tested first, reviewed before merge) and every browser test for all five games on Android and
  iPhone (complete run on this candidate). Privacy checks run on every room screen.
- Jev: 200 games against the real rules (Village won 115, Mafia 85; fair at 6 to 16 players) and 20 evenings played on
  screen with a privacy check on 2,281 room screens: no slips.
- Three pretend players (a first-time host with eight players, a distracted host who reloaded and had players leave, a
  grandparent with weak English on a small phone) played whole evenings; their findings are fixed in this candidate.

## Known, next list
- Wording for weak-English readers ("goes out", "Ghost guess", "Everyone in", some story jokes); role names in Tamil.
- A tap the role can't make gives no feedback (by design, for privacy); the Police's locked first tap isn't explained.
- No Home on the role card, the night job and the pointing countdown; "Done" appears a moment after a pick.
