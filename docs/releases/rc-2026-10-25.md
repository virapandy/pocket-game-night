# Pocket Game Night 1.5.0 (release candidate rc-2026-10-25)

A new game, **What Am I?**: one player holds the phone to their forehead, everyone else gives clues without saying the word.
Tambola, Impostor and Secret Words play as in 1.4.0.

## For the host
- **What Am I?** on "Host a game": one phone, passed round. Nobody sits out writing words, and nobody has seen them.
- **How it plays:** the guesser holds the phone on their forehead or chest, screen facing the room. Everyone else talks,
  acts or makes the noise, but never says the word. Move the phone down for Got it, up to pass (passing is free), or tap the
  bottom or top half instead. Each mark plays a sound (and a buzz on Android).
- **Hands-free, for grandparents and small children:** stand the phone behind the guesser; a clue giver taps the bottom half
  for Got it, the top half to pass. For the whole game, or for one player on their turn.
- **724 everyday words in eight packs** (Animals, Food, Around the house, Jobs, Sports and cricket, Festivals and places,
  Act it out, Famous Indians) or Mix of all; non-veg food words only when switched on. No word repeats tonight until every
  word has been used.
- **30, 60 or 90 seconds a turn.**
- **Fair scores:** when time's up the room sees what was got and passed, and can move any word marked wrong before saving.
  Undo after saving, until the next turn starts.
- **Game-night moments:** someone joining mid-round, someone leaving (their points stay), someone else going first, Pause
  (a call or the app being hidden pauses by itself), "End Riya's turn now", "Home (game saved)" and resume, a running total,
  Scores in the menu, as many rounds as you like, End the game for the winner (ties shared), Play again, and a summary of
  the night. History keeps each game night.
- **Never records anyone:** no camera, no microphone; nothing leaves the phone.

## Also in this release
- Version 1.5.0. Saved games and sessions from earlier versions still open. What Am I? saves its game nights in the same
  versioned format, tied to the word list's edition, so a later word list never changes an old night.
- "What shall we play?" has "← Home" for every game; Home's Host line names all four games.

## Testing for this release
- Every rule test, and every browser test for all four games on Android and iPhone: complete run 40149438240, green.
- What Am I?'s word order, marking, scores and saved game nights were tested first (C3) and passed the AI reviewer before
  merge; 14 extra checks that a "Got it!" always counts (double taps, Pause during the flash, Home, hide and reload).
- Three pretend players (a first-time host with five players, a grandparent with weak English on a small phone, a
  distracted host who reloaded, went back, removed and added players and ended early) played whole evenings on the
  preview: never stuck, every score correct. Their eleven layout findings are fixed in this candidate.

## Known, next list (1.5.1)
- The word screen doesn't show which half is Got it and which is Pass (the hint is on the screen before the turn).
- Taps in the 1.4 s after a mark are ignored by design; players can think a tap was lost.
- Wording for weak-English readers ("Hands-free", "Top so far", "Marked wrong?", "Read this aloud"); "Answer by moving the
  phone?" has no Home; a word hidden by Pause or a reload comes back later without saying so.
- Mafia (game five) is built and tested but not shown; it is released after its three privacy changes.
