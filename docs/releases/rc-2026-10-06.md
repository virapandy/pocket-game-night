# Pocket Game Night 1.3.0 (release candidate rc-2026-10-06)

**New game: Impostor.** One phone, passed round the table: everyone but one player gets the same secret word; the
impostor has to blend in. Built from scenarios v3.9 with decisions I1–I28, after the owner's own play, seven player runs and two
product owner and UX designer reviews. Tambola plays exactly as in 1.2.0.

## For the host
- **Choosing a game:** "Host a game" now opens "What shall we play?" with Tambola and Impostor. Home shows an
  unfinished Impostor game as a row ("Impostor · Riya, Arjun +2 · round 4") to resume.
- **Setting up:** "Who's playing?" (3–20 names in seat order; today's names filled in), then "How do you want to
  play?": audience, Easy or Hard, Free flow or a 2-minute timer, score or no score, categories, and under
  "More options ›" the last-chance guess (off by default). "Same as last time" when the choices carry over.
  "How to play" is a button, never shown by itself; its text follows the chosen settings.
- **Passing the phone:** "Player 2 of 4 / Pass the phone to ARJUN / Everyone else, look away!". Hold the big pad to
  see your word; let go and it hides. Nothing on the screen moves after the first hold. "Don't know this word?"
  asks "New word for everyone?" first. "Tap instead" for anyone who can't hold.
- **Clues and talk:** who starts and the order round the table; "Go round again" (under "Not enough clues?") for 3 to 5
  players; "See my word again" on the clues and talk screens; Free flow or the 2-minute timer with Pause and "1 more minute".
- **The vote:** "Get ready to point… 3, 2, 1, Point!", then tap who got the most fingers and "Reveal Arjun".
  "It's a tie" ("Tap everyone who is tied") gives one re-vote; "Count again" reruns the countdown.
- **One result screen:** "✓ Caught!" or "✗ Escaped!" ("You caught the impostor!", "Arjun wins the round!"), who the
  impostor was, the word and its category, points if kept, then "Next round" with "End game" beside it, "← Home" (game
  saved) and "Players (5) ›". With the last-chance guess on, the impostor guesses first and the room taps right or wrong.
- **End of the game:** "That's the game!" with the winner line, fun lines and main "Play again" (same players and
  choices), then "Play something else" (the names go into Tambola's setup), "Home", "More ›" (Share, History, "Oops,
  keep playing", Discard).
- **People coming and going:** add someone mid-round ("Joining next round: Zoya"); someone leaving gets "Finish this
  round first" or "Deal again without Kabir" (never reveals the impostor); "3 players needed." below three.
- **Breaks and slips:** "Home (game is saved)" mid-round; "Not Riya? ← Back" if the phone went to the wrong person,
  also in "See my word again"; a half-second guard against double taps; settings kept on Back; "Oops, keep playing"
  visible on the end screen; "Cancel" when someone is leaving.
- **How to play** starts with "One player is the impostor: they don't know the secret word." Larger text works on
  every Impostor screen.
- **Words:** 291 words in 9 categories, Multicultural list; non-veg optional; no word repeats in a game; recent
  games' words avoided; "This word didn't work" skips a word for good.
- **Interruptions:** close the app, take a call, come back: the game reopens where it was. Rounds left more than
  3 hours ask to deal again; games end by themselves after 12 hours.

## Privacy and fairness
- The secret word is in the page only while the pad is held; the impostor's turn looks exactly like everyone
  else's. The host screen never shows the word or the impostor before the reveal.
- The word, the impostor and the starter come from their own seeds, kept on this phone; nobody is impostor three
  rounds running; in Hard mode the impostor never starts.

## Also in this release
- Version 1.3.0. Saved Tambola games, tickets and sessions from 1.0.0–1.2.0 still open. Impostor games from
  earlier preview builds that can't replay are hidden, never deleted (they never reached the families' link).
- Screen readers hear the clue order, the result lines and the word; the announcer starts empty at each deal.
- Everywhere on screen it is a "game" (never "evening", "night" or "session").
