# Pocket Game Night 1.1.0 (release candidate rc-2026-10-03b)

The UX list of 1–3 October (rows 1–25), built in three parallel lanes plus one core batch. Every row was checked
by the reviewer before merging; the core rows were tested first. Families had 1.0.0 (1 October) until now.

## For the host
- **Home:** two equal choices, "Host a game" and "Join with my ticket"; unfinished games as plain rows (row 2 of 1 Oct).
- **Paper or phone tickets:** two equal cards with their explanation, neither chosen in advance (row 3 of 1 Oct).
  In landscape the cards sit side by side (row 11).
- **First game of all:** no separate "New session" screen; the session line above "Confirm prizes" is enough (row 9).
- **Hand-out:** "Game 7K3P" above the QR, "Check it says Game 7K3P"; buttons full width at the bottom; "Can't scan?
  Give a paper ticket" as a link (rows 10, 25). "Start calling" on the last ticket asks once if that player has
  their ticket (row 7). Giving a paper ticket shows "Kabir plays on paper · Undo" until the first call (row 8).
- **Calling:** "Tambola · Game 7K3P" in the top bar and on the room view (row 25); better landscape layout with a
  72 px "Next number" (row 11); prize chips wrap (row 19); screen readers hear each number, rhyme and verdict (row 4).
- **Claims:** one main button per screen (row 5); in phone-ticket games "Add another winner" works while a win waits
  (row 6); every scanned verdict shows its proof, "Ticket 3 · game 7K3P · same numbers as your copy" (row 24);
  a claim from an old game gets a calm note, "That game has ended…" (row 23).
- **End of game:** a "Game over" banner above the payouts (row 22); "gets ₹35 / pays ₹12" stays on one line (row 14).
- **Settings during a game:** "Back" at the top (row 18). **History:** clearer wording for one past game (row 19).

## For players (phone tickets)
- Tickets fit narrow phones, down to 320 px (row 2); 16 px side margins (row 19).
- Marks are deep blue with a ✓, not red; the pattern outline is orange with a corner mark (row 3).
- The optional "marks fill a pattern" hint stays one line, names prizes ("Ticket 1: Early Five and Top Line
  filled"), says Early Five once, with "More" for the rest (rows 1, 11).
- "Which ticket?" shows small pictures; the claim QR screen outlines only the chosen prize (rows 12, 13).
- "Done with this game…" clears this phone's tickets after asking (row 20); tickets from more than 6 hours ago wait
  on Home with Open and Clear (row 21); a new game's ticket says "Your tickets from game 7K3P were cleared" (row 23).

## Small phones and landscape (fixed during the release check)
- Screenshot comparison at 360×640, 390×844 and 812×375 found layout problems before families saw them; fixed: Quick mark fits a 360 px phone with "Show claim" on screen; hand-out QR no longer covers buttons in landscape; the scanned verdict no longer overlaps the prize chips; the big number, payouts, prizes, room view and setup title fit small and sideways phones; the iPhone install tip's "Got it" is no longer a main button.
