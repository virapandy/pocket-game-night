# Pocket Game Night 1.6.2 (release candidate rc-2026-10-27)

Clearer words and labels in What Am I? and Mafia, from the pretend players' walks. Tambola, Impostor and Secret Words play
as in 1.6.1.

## What Am I?
- **"Pass" and "Got it" on the word screen:** small labels at the top and bottom show which half is which, in every way of
  holding the phone and after the screen turns. Tapping a label counts for its half. The word uses the space between them.
- **"Stand the phone up"** replaces "Hands-free" everywhere, and How to play explains it.
- **Plainer score lines:** "A word in the wrong list? Tap it to move it." and "Most so far: Arjun 14".

## Mafia
- **Plainer words:** "who is out" instead of "goes out"; "Your guess: who is Mafia?" instead of "Ghost guess"; "Everyone
  still playing: point at who you think is Mafia."
- **The Villager's line:** "You think it's Arjun. The best Mafia spotter is named at the end."
- **How to play** says what to do if you need to stop during someone's turn.
- **Game over:** "Change players or roles" and "← Home" scroll with the list; "Play again" and "End for tonight" stay at
  the bottom.
- Every role's night screen still looks the same from across the table.

## Every game
- A game's name never breaks across two lines (for example "What Am I?" on a sideways phone).

## Testing for this release
- Every rule test and every browser test for all five games on Android and iPhone (complete run on this candidate).
- New checks for the labels at every size, both holds and after turning; the new wording; game names on one line on Home,
  Join, the picker, Home rows, History and summaries; and Mafia's same-looking night screens with the new lines. Both
  changes passed the AI reviewer.

## Known, next list
- Mafia: no Home on the role card, the night job and the pointing countdown (kept by design); "Done" appears a moment after a
  pick.
- What Am I?: taps in the 1.4 s after a mark are ignored (kept by design).
