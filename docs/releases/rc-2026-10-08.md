# Pocket Game Night 1.3.1 (release candidate rc-2026-10-08)

Small fixes to Impostor from the player re-run after 1.3.0 (decisions I29 and I30). Tambola plays as in 1.3.0.

## For the host
- **Menus fit the screen:** the in-game "··· Menu" and the end screen's "More" list scroll when there isn't room, so
  "End game" is always reachable, also with the phone sideways or on the smallest phones (R1).
- **No double taps:** for half a second after any screen, dialog or sheet appears, taps on its buttons are ignored, so a
  quick double tap never skips a step. This now covers every Impostor screen, Home and "What shall we play?". The hold
  pad and typing are never blocked (R2).
- **No accidental text selection** when tapping; typing in name boxes still works (R3).
- **Option labels keep their spaces** ("Whole family", "Free flow"), still on one line on small phones (R4).
- **Home:** the second card is now "Join a game", with "Tambola ticket from the host"; the Join screen says "Join a game".

## Also in this release
- Version 1.3.1. Saved games, tickets and sessions from earlier versions still open; nothing about saved data changed.

  (today's names after going Home) and R10 (fast and slow Enter) pass. TAM-112 (Tambola after Android closes Chrome)
  passes on the Android emulator (weekly run 37593615495). Every change passed the AI reviewer before merge.
