# Pocket Game Night 1.6.1 (release candidate rc-2026-10-26c)

A small Mafia update. Tambola, Impostor, Secret Words and What Am I? play as in 1.6.0.

## For the host
- **Mafia: the night screen says why a name can't be picked.** In 1.6.0 a tap on a name your role may not pick did
  nothing, which felt broken. Now a short line shows, only to the player holding the phone, in the same place on every
  role's screen: "That's you. Pick someone else.", "Zoya is Mafia too. Pick someone else.", "You protected Riya last night.
  Pick someone else.", "You've already protected yourself once. Pick someone else.", "You checked Arjun on night 2: Mafia.",
  "Tonight's check is done: …", or that the player is out. Nothing is picked and nothing changes for anyone else.

## Privacy
- Every role's night screen still looks the same from across the table: the line sits in the same box, the same size, for
  every role, whether or not it shows. It never appears on a screen the room sees, makes no sound and no buzz, and goes
  when the job is hidden.

## Testing for this release
- Every rule test and every browser test for all five games on Android and iPhone (complete run on this candidate).
- New checks for each of the seven lines, for every role, and that every role's night screen keeps the same boxes and sizes
  with and without a line, at 320 × 568 and 390 × 844, with Larger text. Passed the AI reviewer before merge.

## Known, next list
- As in 1.6.0: wording for weak-English readers ("goes out", "Ghost guess", "Everyone in", some story jokes); no Home on
  the role card, the night job and the pointing countdown; "Done" appears a moment after a pick.
