# Pocket Game Night 1.5.1 (release candidate rc-2026-10-25b)

A quick fix for What Am I?. Tambola, Impostor and Secret Words play as in 1.5.0.

## Fixed
- **What Am I?: a rare blank screen at the end of a turn.** If Got it or Pass was tapped at the exact moment the time ran
  out, the screen could go blank, with only Home or a reload to get out. Now "Time's up!" always shows, and the word counts
  once. Found by 40 simulated game nights played by Jev.

## Testing for this release
- Every rule test and every browser test for all four games on Android and iPhone (complete run on this candidate).
- A new check taps at the last moment (29,399, 29,400 and 29,401 ms of a 30 s turn) and expects "Time's up!" with the right
  lists and score; it failed before the fix. The fix passed the AI reviewer.
- Jev simulations since 1.5.0: 40 What Am I? evenings (4,456 marks, 39 reloads): every score and saved night correct.

## Known, next list
- As in 1.5.0: the word screen doesn't show which half is Got it and which is Pass; taps in the 1.4 s after a mark are
  ignored by design; wording for weak-English readers; "Answer by moving the phone?" has no Home.
- Mafia (game five) is built and tested but not shown yet.
