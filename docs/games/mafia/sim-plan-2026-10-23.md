# Mafia: simulated table games, the plan (product owner, 23 October 2026)

Owner, 23 October: "start simulation". Since 23 October checks before code are the product side's job (handover, "Jev for
design checks"), the product owner runs this, not the tester. There is no Mafia rules engine yet (Mafia is built after What Am
I?), so the referee is a small script in the product owner's scratchpad, never in the repo.

## Why AI players, not the rough model
The balance check (`balance-2026-10-23.md`) used a Village that votes at random, which real families beat; that is why MF16
and MF17 were dropped. AI players that talk, accuse, claim and vote are closer to a real table, though still not humans:
the result is evidence of **how the rules behave with reasoning players**, not of what families will feel.

## The set-up
- **Each player is a separate AI helper** that knows only its own role (and, for Mafia, its partners) and what the room
  has seen. It never sees another player's role or night pick.
- **A referee script** deals roles from a seed and applies MF1–MF15 exactly: night first; the night pass in seat order
  shifting each night; Mafia agree (first picks, others agree or change, random if they differ); Doctor (once on
  themselves, never the same player twice running); Police (Mafia or Not Mafia, private); role shown when voted out; tie →
  point again once → nobody out; "Nobody today"; Mafia win when equal to the rest.
- **Each day:** every player still in says up to two sentences in seat order (they may claim a role or lie), then everyone
  points at once. **Each night:** every player does their night job, Villagers pick who they think is Mafia, out players
  make a ghost guess.
- **A watcher helper** sees only what the room sees (who was out, roles shown, the talk, the votes) and guesses each living
  player's role after every day: a measure of how well the Mafia hide.
- **Games:** 12 in all, on the convention table (MF5): 3 at 6 players, 4 at 8, 3 at 10, 2 at 12. Mid-size model for players.

## What the report says (`sim-table-2026-10-xx.md`)
Village wins by player count, against the convention's expectation; nights and days per game; how many days a player who
goes out first sits out; how often the Doctor saves; how often the Mafia disagree; ties and "Nobody today"; whether the
Police claims and what happens after; the watcher's accuracy; rules any player got wrong or asked about; cost.

## Cost and limits
About 40 helper turns a game, about 500 in all; roughly 3 to 5 million tokens and an hour or two. Limits: AI players are not
families (they don't laugh, get bored or peek); 12 games show patterns, not exact win rates.
