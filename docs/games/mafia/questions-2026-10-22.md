| # | Question | Options | Recommendation |

| M1 | **How does night work with one phone?** (no app we found does A: new, so the persona agents walk it on the prototype before scenarios) | **A. Pass the phone round at night:** everyone takes it in turn; Mafia pick someone to put out, Doctor picks someone to protect, Police picks someone to check, Villagers tap anyone (a "who do you suspect?" pick). Same time and taps for every role, so nobody can tell. No closing eyes, works with kids and noise · **B.
