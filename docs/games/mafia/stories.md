# Mafia: the morning stories (draft v1, 23 October 2026)

The list is `stories.csv` (edition 1: 15 "out" stories, 11 "saved" stories). The host reads one aloud each morning
(screen map M1). The phone then adds the role line ("Kabir was a Villager.") and the count line ("7 players left · Mafia
caught: 0 of 2").

## Rules for a story
1. **Funny, never frightening.** Nobody is hurt, killed, shot or harmed; people are sent away, locked in, sent on long
   trips, or fall asleep. "Out" is the only word for leaving the game (MF2).
2. **A saved story never names anyone**: not who was saved (the Mafia never pick their own, so it would clear that
   player) and never the Doctor's player. It always ends "Nobody is out!".
3. **An "out" story names only the player who is out** (`{NAME}`, twice at most) and ends "{NAME} is out."
4. **English words, or Indian words known everywhere** (samosa, jalebi, auto, chai, cricket); the Tirunelveli test of
   `docs/games/secret-words/words.md` rule 3. No politics, religion, caste, real people, brands, alcohol.
5. **Short:** at most 25 words, so it fits the morning screen at 320 × 568 without scrolling and reads aloud in about
   ten seconds.
6. **Never the same story twice in a game**; chosen at random from the game's seed (screen map M1). With 15 "out" and 11
   "saved" stories, a game of up to 16 players never runs out.
