# Mafia: what the simulated table games showed (product owner, 23 October 2026)

Plan: `sim-plan-2026-10-23.md` (owner asked, 23 October). 12 games on the convention table (MF5): 3 at 6 players, 4 at 8, 3
at 10, 2 at 12. Each player was a separate AI helper (mid-size model) that knew only its own role and what the room had seen,
with a persona from the player cast; a referee in the workflow script applied MF1–MF15; a watcher saw only the room.
**Not played** (they don't change who wins): Villagers' night picks and out players' ghost guesses. **Limits:** AI players are
not families: they reason carefully, remember every point, never get bored, laugh or peek. Twelve games show patterns, not
exact chances.

## What it says
| Finding | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **The Village won 11 of 12.** On day 1 only about a third of the Village's points landed on a Mafia (26 of 72), but from day 2 on most did; the watcher found the Mafia from day 2 in most games | With reasoning players the room's public pointing and the roles shown on the way out give the Village a lot to work with. The rough model (random votes) said the opposite. Real families sit somewhere between: AI Mafia lie badly and AI Villagers never forget who pointed at whom | **No rule change** on AI evidence alone (`evidence-2026-10-23.md`); MF5's table stays. Watch the Village's win rate in families' first games |
| **Games are short**: 1–2 rounds at 6 players, 2–3 at 8, 3–4 at 10, 4–6 at 12 | Matches the guide's 15–30 minutes | none |
| **The first player out sits out 1 to 6 rounds** (4–6 at 12 players) | The classic boredom complaint is real at 12 | Supports MF11 (the ghost guess); keep it |
| **The Mafia never disagreed** at night (0 of 21 nights with two or three Mafia) | The "first picks, others agree or change" step (MF1b) is simple enough | none |
| **The Doctor saved someone on 10 of 36 nights** | The Doctor matters without dominating | none |
| **4 ties, all settled by pointing again; no second tie; "Nobody today" never chosen** | MF6 works; "Nobody today" is rarely wanted | none |
| **The Police claimed in 6 games**, and was usually out the next night; twice a Mafia claimed a role falsely and was caught | The normal Mafia story: claiming is a choice, and the rules leave it to the room | none |
| **No player asked about a rule; no invalid pick in 393 turns** | MF1–MF15, as written in one paragraph, were enough to play | The rules text used is a good base for How to play (S4) |

## Cost
393 helper turns, **about 22 million tokens** (each turn carried the rules and the whole game so far), 8½ minutes. The plan
estimated 3–5 million: **about five times over**. A rerun should cut each turn's context (a short summary of earlier days
instead of every line) and use fewer, larger games.
