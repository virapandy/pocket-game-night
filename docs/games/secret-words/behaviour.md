# Secret Words: behaviour around the phone (4 October 2026)

Product owner, on the owner's request to "look more deeply on the behaviour aspects". Inputs: research on social behaviour in
party games (alpha players, tells, kids and losing, generations, Indian family gatherings; sources below), a simulation of three
evenings (a 12-person joint-family Diwali, a friends' Saturday with couples, a weeknight with a 7- and a 13-year-old), and a
player walk from two quiet perspectives (Divya, a shy new daughter-in-law; Nana, 78, proud and slow to read).
**[Inference]** marks our reasoning where no source exists (deference to elders during games, grabbing a shared phone).

## 1. What happens to people around one phone
| # | Behaviour | Seen in | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Whoever holds the phone decides.** The loud uncle taps every Reveal; a whispered right answer ("Diya?") is never heard | Diwali; research on "quarterbacking" | Quiet players, elders, kids and spouses lose their say; the evening becomes one person's game |
| H2 | **The same person always gives clues**, overriding the rotation; the summary praises only them | Friends' night | Others never get the "spy" role, which every player said is the most fun |
| H3 | **Being made clue giver by the machine, in public, feels like a test** | Nana; Dadaji handed the phone to his son and gave no more clues | Elders lose face; research: public judgement of someone's reasoning is the biggest emotional risk |
| H4 | **A loss lands on one person.** The 8-year-old who tapped the Landmine cried in the kitchen and "isn't playing that again" | Diwali; research on kids 6–8 | Children learn to lose from small, kind losses; a named, dramatic loss teaches them to quit |
| H5 | **Face-tell accusations and one-sided penalties.** "He smiled!" → "Clue broke a rule?" pressed for a face, 10 minutes of half-real outrage | Friends' night | The only dispute tool punishes at once; no shared rule says "poker face" to everyone |
| H6 | **Nobody contradicts an elder; juniors stay silent** | Divya; research on joint families | The right answer goes unsaid; the game rewards hierarchy, not ideas [inference] |
| H7 | **The waiting team has nothing to do** while a clue is thought up (1–4 minutes); teens pick up their own phones | All three | Energy dies; research: long pauses are the top complaint |
| H8 | **Kids who read slowly stop trying**; a "Guesses only" kid feels shut out ("Why can't I see the map?") | Weeknight | Kids love tapping and secrets; reading speed shouldn't decide who plays |
| H9 | **The evening ends on a low**: a dispute, a tear, or a bare stats screen | Friends' night, weeknight | Research (peak-end rule): people remember the best moment and the ending |
| H10 | **Couples on one team bicker**; shuffles can stack all the kids or all the strong players on one side | Friends' night; research | Fairness feels personal between relatives |
| H11 | **Women serving tea join late or not at all** | Research on Indian gatherings | Joining mid-game must be effortless and unremarked |
| H12 | **Positive naming lands well; negative naming hurts.** Divya liked that a wrong guess named no one; Nana wanted "Nana's clue found 2!" | Divya, Nana | Credit people, blame the board |

## 2. What the phone does about it (proposals)
| # | Proposal | Fixes | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **A rotating "picker"** for each turn, named on the board: "Sunita picks this turn". The main reads "Sunita: Reveal LAMP". Rotates through each team's guessers in list order, so everyone, kids and elders included, gets the deciding tap; "Pass the pick" hands it to someone else. The team still talks; the picker taps | H1, H6, H8 | **Owner question QA** |
| B2 | **Two clue givers can share the role** ("Nana + Aarav give clues"): one reads the map, the other speaks or types. Counts for both in the rotation | H3, H8 | **Owner question QB** |
| B3 | **A kinder dispute box.** "Clue not allowed?" offers three equal choices: "Let it go" · "Try another clue" (the clue giver gives a new clue, same turn) · "Turn ends, they get a word" (the standard penalty), with the line "Both clue givers decide together." | H5 | **Owner question QC** |
| B4 | **Ask, don't assign.** Game over shows "Next clue givers: Meena and Kabir" with "Swap"; "Doesn't give clues" is set privately in the Clue givers sheet and never shown on Make teams | H2, H3 | Ours |
| B5 | **Credit people, blame the board.** Own word after a clue: "✓ Nana's clue works! 2 guesses left."; a wrong word never names a person; the Landmine: "Boom! The Landmine got Chai Champions."; the summary credits every clue giver ("Clues tonight from Riya, Nana and Zoya") instead of ranking one; no Landmine count | H2, H4, H12 | Ours |
| B6 | **A shared poker-face rule.** At the start of each guessing turn the board strip shows "Riya: poker face, no hints!" for 3 s, so nobody has to accuse anyone | H5 | Ours |
| B7 | **A job for the waiting team.** On the pass screen and while guessing: "Coffee Commandos: guess how many Riya will say!" | H7 | Ours |
| B8 | **End on a high.** Game over adds a near-miss line ("Coffee Commandos were 1 word away!") and, when clue words were typed, the best clue ("MONSOON 3: all 3 found"); the summary leads with a shared line ("6 players · 3 games · 31 words found") before the tally; after a loss "Play again" reads "Rematch!" | H9, H4 | Ours |

## 3. What the phone must never do
- **Name a person for a mistake** (no "Ishaan hit the Landmine", no per-person error counts).
- **Police faces or talk** (no camera, microphone or "cheating detected"); fairness comes from shared rules on screen.
- **Nag**: each tip shows at most once per evening; "poker face" is a 3-second line, not a popup.
- **Force rotation**: suggest, allow a one-tap swap; the phone never overrules a grandmother.
- **Show public opt-out labels**, "slowest clue" or "longest turn" stats.
- **Pressure people to keep playing**; "End for tonight" is never hidden.
- **Keep winner leaderboards beyond the evening** unless the host opts in (a season scoreboard stays a later idea).
- **Replace the room**: no voting on screens, no per-player phones for guessing; the talk is the game.

## 4. Questions for the owner
| # | Question | Options | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| QA | A rotating **picker** who makes the final tap each turn | On for every game · a setup choice, off by default · no | **On for every game**: it is the single biggest fix for one person taking over |
| QB | **Pairs of clue givers** (an elder or child with a partner) | Allowed · not in the first release | **Allowed**, set in the Clue givers sheet |
| QC | The **dispute box** | Three choices "Let it go / Try another clue / Turn ends, they get a word" · keep only the standard penalty | **Three choices** |
