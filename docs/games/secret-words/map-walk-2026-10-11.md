# Secret Words: six persona walks of the screen map (product owner, 11 October 2026)

**What this is.** Stage 1, step 3 of `docs/proposals/design-before-code.md`: six helpers, each playing one person from the
player cast, walked `screen-map.md` draft 1 from its on-screen words and buttons only (no scenarios, guide, decisions or
guidelines), one after another, on a mid-size model (owner, 11 October: right-size the model). The product owner merged
the six reports into this one list. **Nothing in the map or the scenarios changes until the owner answers section 3.**
Jev's per-screen and per-label read (handover "DO NOW", tester) will be merged into the same list when it arrives.

**design** = a conscious decision the personas disliked (the owner confirms or changes it).

## 3. Decisions for the owner (all at once; nothing changes before these)
1. **Help (gap 1):** quiet "How to play" on Choices, quiet "Clue rules" on the map phone. Yes/no.
2. **Visible tap guards (gap 2):** main buttons show greyed for their guard, then come alive. Yes/no; K35 timings unchanged.
3. **Where-line (gap 3):** one line on "Welcome back.", the Pause cover and the resume row saying turn, team and step. Yes/no.
4. **Reveal stays final (gap 4):** confirm K35, or allow an Undo within 10 s for the picker only. Rule decision.
5. **The QR on the table (gap 6):** A reword only, or B QR behind "Show the code" that hides after 60 s.
6. **Visible Pause (gap 7):** a Pause icon beside the menu on pass, clue and board screens; menu grouped. Yes/no.
7. **Map phone in step (gap 8):** "I give clues for …" highlight on three phones; "Next map" asks first. Yes/no each.
8. **Wording batch (gaps 9, 10, 12, 13):** approve as one C1 batch after Jev's label list arrives, or now.

## 4. What the walks cost
Six runs on a mid-size model, about 20 s to 75 s each, roughly 440,000 tokens in all, no browser. Draft 1 of the map took
about an hour to write. Compare: Impostor's three AI player runs after the build took about 45 minutes and several hundred
tool calls, and found problems that then cost code and test rounds.
