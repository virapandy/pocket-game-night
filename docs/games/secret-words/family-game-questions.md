# Secret Words: the first family game on the preview (owner, before the families' release)

Play one evening on the preview link with at least 4 people, two or three games, with whichever phones you have. Afterwards,
answer these five in a line or two each. Nothing else is needed; the product owner turns the answers into changes.

1. **Getting going.** How long from opening the app to the first clue? Did anyone need you to explain what to tap?
2. **The secret map.** Did any guesser see the map by accident, or did a clue giver struggle to see or hide it?
3. **Reading the board.** Could everyone read the words? Did anyone use the tap-to-zoom? Any word nobody knew?
4. **Stuck or wrong.** Was there any moment the game didn't do what people expected, or someone tapped and nothing happened?
5. **Again?** Would this group play it again next time? What one thing would make it better?

If something went badly wrong, a photo of the screen helps more than a description.
