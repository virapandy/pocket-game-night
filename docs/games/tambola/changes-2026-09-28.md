# Tambola change request, 28 September 2026 (from the owner's first feedback on Phase 1a)

For the orchestrator: ask the tester to apply these scenario changes in `specs/tambola/`, update the tests,
then run the loop. Everything here is **owner-approved** unless marked "to confirm".

## 1. Unclaimed prize money goes back to everyone
Owner: "End game with one tier won gives the entire cash to the winners, but the remaining cash should be
distributed equally to everyone."

**Replace TAM-088** with:
> ## TAM-088: Money from prizes nobody won goes back to the players
> Given the game ends with some tiers unclaimed (for example only Early Five was won)
> Then the money of every unclaimed tier is handed back to the players, equally per ticket
> (a player with 2 tickets gets twice as much back as a player with 1), split to the rupee
> And the winners of claimed tiers get exactly their tier amounts, nothing more
> And payouts plus money handed back add up to the pot exactly
> And the payout summary shows, per person: paid, won, handed back, and the net amount

**Change TAM-144** to say it is the special case of TAM-088: nobody won, so all the money goes back.
**Check TAM-066, TAM-089, TAM-140, PLT-017** still read correctly ("unclaimed tiers follow TAM-088").

*To confirm with the owner:* "equally" is read as **equal per ticket** (everyone gets back in proportion to
what they paid). If the owner means equal per person regardless of tickets, change that one line.

## 2. Paper tickets: trust the anchor, no number check
Owner: "Claim in paper mode can be entirely offline without having to verify; trust the anchor."

**Replace TAM-037** with:
> ## TAM-037: Paper tickets: the anchor checks the ticket, the host records the win
> Given the game uses paper tickets
> When a player shouts a claim and the anchor checks their ticket in front of the room
> Then the host taps "Record a win", picks the prize and the winning player (several players for a tie),
> and confirms; no numbers are typed
> And the app shows "Top Line: ✓ Riya, ₹50" for the room
> And if the anchor rules it a bogey, the host can record "Bogey: Riya" (it appears in the summary)

**Change TAM-038:** the "late claim" check applies to phone tickets only; with paper tickets the anchor judges.
**TAM-039** (host picks the player) stays, as part of "Record a win".
**TAM-034, TAM-035, TAM-036** (the app judging claims) apply to phone tickets only from now on.
The typed-number check that is already built may stay as an optional "Check numbers" helper in the menu
for disputes, off the main path.

## 3. Phone tickets: the player's phone shows a claim QR, the anchor scans it
Owner: "In device mode, each device can generate a QR which the anchor can scan and quickly verify."

**New TAM-177** (Phase 2):
> ## TAM-177: A phone-ticket claim is verified by scanning the player's claim QR
> Given Riya holds ticket 3 on her phone
> When she shouts "Top Line!" and taps "Show claim" on her phone, picking Top Line
> Then her phone shows a claim QR carrying her ticket number, the game code and the prize claimed
> When the host taps "Scan a claim" and points the host phone at it
> Then the verdict appears within 2 seconds, with no internet: "Top Line: ✓ Accepted, ₹50 to Riya"
> or "✗ Bogey: 72 not called"
> And the host phone checks the ticket against its own copy (TAM-055), so an edited QR is refused

**Change TAM-174:** entering the ticket number by hand stays as the fallback when scanning fails.
**TAM-060 still holds:** the player shouts first; the QR only replaces typing, not the shout.

## 4. The calling screen and setup layout
Owner: "Screen layout can be better, especially the callout screen."
Apply `docs/games/tambola/ux-calling-screen.md`: one screen with no scrolling, the number dominant, a menu
for End game and Discard, prize chips, the board in a sheet, an undo toast that never moves anything, and
the main button fixed at the bottom on every setup step.

**Change TAM-082:** tiers with the same share always get the same amount; rounding differences go to
Full House first.
**New TAM-138** (Phase 1a): the calling screen needs no scrolling on a 390 × 844 screen, and the called
number stays fully visible after calls, claims, undo and closing a tier.
