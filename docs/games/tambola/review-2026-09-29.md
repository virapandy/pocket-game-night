# Product owner review of the live 1a.1 build, 29 September 2026

Walked through https://virapandy.github.io/pocket-game-night/ at 390 × 844 (Android-sized): setup with
6 paper tickets at ₹50, calling, recording a win, closing the prize, ending the game.

## What works well
- **Setup:** the main button is fixed at the bottom of every step; the contribution has a real ₹50 default
  and shows "Pot: ₹300 (6 tickets × ₹50)"; the prizes step fits on one screen; the three Lines are equal.
- **Calling screen:** no scrolling (page height = screen height); the number dominates; slim top bar with
  "3 of 90 called" and the menu; Repeat and Another rhyme as quiet buttons; last calls; prize chips;
  "Record a win" in its own row above a full-width "Next number". The screen-sleep hint is a one-time tip.
- **Record a win:** prize, then player(s): several can be picked for a tie, Bogey is one tap, Back and
  Cancel are there. The result "Early Five: ✓ Player 2, ₹40", "Close Early Five first" on the main button,
  and a Close button on the prize chip all read clearly.
- **Payouts:** correct under the new rule. Pot ₹300: ₹40 in prizes, ₹260 handed back equally per ticket
  (₹44, ₹44, ₹43, ₹43, ₹43, ₹43), with paid, won, handed back and net per person.

## Findings to fix (in order of importance)
| # | Finding | Scenario it breaks or misses | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Early Five is ₹40, not ₹30.** 6 tickets, ₹300 pot, 10/15/15/15/45: the Lines round down to ₹40 each (right), but the leftover went to Early Five (₹40) and Full House got ₹140 instead of ₹150. Early Five now equals a Line, losing "small prizes first". | TAM-082 ("rounding differences go to Full House first"); the existing test didn't catch it | Every tier except Full House is its exact share rounded **down** to the unit (tiers with equal shares stay equal); Full House takes everything left. Tester: add a property check for this across many pots and ticket counts. |
| 2 | **The win card covers the called number.** After "Record a win", a card over the top half hides the number until the host taps Done, even after the prize is closed. | TAM-138 and TAM-123 ("the number stays fully visible after … a claim … and closing a tier") | Show the verdict where the rhyme is (the number shrinks a little but stays visible), or as a band below the number; after "Close", the card goes away by itself. |
| 3 | **The undo toast covers the prize chips** for 5 seconds after every call, so with auto-call (1b, every 10 seconds) the chips are hidden half the time. | TAM-125 only protects "Next number" and "Record a win" | Change TAM-125: the toast also never covers the prize chips; place it over the empty space between the rhyme and the last calls. |
| 4 | After a prize is closed, the verdict card stays until "Done" is tapped. | TAM-145 flow; minor | Close removes the card; Done is not needed after Close. |
