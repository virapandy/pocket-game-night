# Word Link (working name): the whole evening, stage by stage (draft, 4 October 2026)

By the five stages of `docs/proposals/next-game-lifecycle.md`, using what Impostor taught us (`docs/spec-rules.md`,
guidelines 45–48, 45a, 46a). Rules in `guide.md`.

## Who is involved
| Role | Who | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Sets up; holds phone 1 between turns; also plays | |
| Clue-giver (one per team) | Sees the key; gives one-word clues | Hardest role: the app keeps it calm (no timer by default) |
| Guessers | Discuss and tap words | |
| Newcomer | Never played | Plays as a guesser first; how to play on request |
| Guest with the link | Has a phone | Can become the clue-givers' key phone with a code |

## Ways to play
| Mode | First release? | Falls back to |
|---|---|---|
| Two phones: board + clue-givers' key phone (code, offline) | Yes (recommended) | One phone |
| One phone: board, clue-giver peeks before each clue | Yes | Paper: write the grid, the phone keeps the key |
| Players' own phones (connected) | Later | |

## Stage 1. Decide
"What shall we play?" gets a third card: "Word Link · 4–12 players in two teams · about 15 min · Find your team's words".

## Stage 2. Set up
1. **Teams:** names from tonight's session; two columns, tap a name to move it across; "Shuffle teams". Each team picks
   its clue-giver (tap the ★). Team names and colours are ours (not red/blue), with a pattern each.
2. **Choices** (remembered, same pattern as Impostor): Board **Easy 4 × 4** / Standard 5 × 5; Danger word **Normal** /
   Gentle (Standard only); Words **Whole family** / + Grown-ups; Timer **Off** / 2-minute nudge.
3. **Key phone:** "Clue-givers have a second phone?" Yes → a 6-character code and a QR to open on that phone;
   No → one-phone peeks.
Target: 60 s first time, 20 s for a group that played tonight.

## Stage 3. Teach (on request only)
"How to play" on the choices screen and in the menu: five lines plus "A good first clue links 2 words." A first-game
tip line on the clue-giver's key: "Pick 2 of your words that go together. Say one word that links them, and 2."

## Stage 4. Play
- **Board (phone 1, face up):** the grid in big type (Easy 4 × 4: about 85 px tiles at 390 px wide); a banner "Team
  Mango's turn" in the team colour; words left per team; "Clue: 3 · guesses left 4".
- **Clue:** the clue-giver says it aloud; on phone 1 they (or anyone) tap the number (1–9) so the phone counts guesses.
- **Guess:** tap a word → "Reveal TIFFIN?" (pick, then confirm, guideline 47) → the tile shows its owner's colour and
  pattern, or "nobody's", or the danger word. "End our turn" is always available after one guess.
- **One-phone peek:** "Pass the phone to RIYA (clue-giver)" → hold to see the key (the same private hold as Impostor:
  key only while held, nothing moves, same screen for both clue-givers) → let go → "Back to the middle".
- **Key phone:** shows the key permanently (it never leaves the clue-givers); it cannot know which words were found,
  so the clue-givers watch the board (later, connected mode can sync).
- **Moments:** a disputed clue (the other clue-giver decides; nothing to tap); undo the last reveal before the next
  clue (pick-then-confirm makes it rare); interruptions resume on the board, never on the key; a dead phone: carry on
  with paper, the key phone still works.

## Stage 5. After
- Game end: "Team Mango wins!" with how (all words found / the other team touched the danger word); the whole key is
  shown on the board for everyone; "Play again" (new board, same teams, the other team starts) and "Swap clue-givers".
- Night: wins per team in tonight's session; fun lines ("Best clue: Riya linked 4 words").
- History: each game's board and key, kept for looking back; no word repeats within an evening where possible.
