# What Am I?: six persona walks of the screen map (product owner, 23 October 2026)

Stage 1, step 3 of `docs/proposals/design-before-code.md`. Six player helpers (mid-size model, right-size rule) each walked
draft 2 of `screen-map.md` from its on-screen words and buttons only (a screens-only extract; no guide, decisions or
rules). Owner asked for the walks and "fix what they find" (23 October); the fixes are section L of the map. Jev's
per-screen and word-list check is in the handover for the tester and will be merged when its report arrives.

| Persona | Worst moment |
|---|---|
| Grandparent, about 70, weak English, shaky hands | T3: can't see how to stop; tiny Pause |
| 10-year-old, fast double taps, wiggles | T3: wiggle marks words; greyed buttons eat taps |
| First-time host, 8 people | R1/S3: no visible rule for when the game ends; S3 says 60 s whatever was chosen |
| Distracted host, doorbell and dinner | T3: Pause unreachable on a forehead (stuck) |
| Competitive uncle | T4: anyone can move words, nothing marked |
| Guest who just opened the link | A1: no way in on their own phone (stuck); missed the rules |

Severity as the players said it: **stuck** · **confused** · **annoyed**. Nobody hit a dead end except the two "stuck" rows.

## Gaps by how many personas hit them, and the fix
| # | Gap, in the players' words | Hit by | Worst | Fix (map section L) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pausing mid-turn**: the only Pause is a tiny corner icon on a phone on someone's forehead | 5/6 | stuck | L1: a clear "Pause" button in the top corner, outside the tap zones, for the room (the screen faces them); laying the phone flat, face up, for 1.5 s pauses; phone Back pauses |
| 2 | **Greyed buttons eat my tap**; I tap again and may hit the next screen | 5/6 | annoyed | L2: keep the shared guard look (all games); a tap during the guard is never carried to the next screen |
| 3 | **Three exits sound alike** (Home (keep this game) / Home (game saved) / End the game / End for tonight); fear of losing scores | 3/6 | confused | L3: one word "Home (game saved)" everywhere; a line under each End saying what it does |
| 4 | **When does the game end?** S3 says "Most words tonight wins"; nothing says who stops | 2/6 | confused | L4: S3 and R1 say "Play as many rounds as you like. Tap End the game to see the winner." |
| 5 | **S3 says 60 seconds** even when 30 or 90 was chosen | 1/6 | confused | L5: S3 uses the chosen length |
| 6 | **A wrong tilt mid-turn** can't be undone | 3/6 | confused | L6: no mid-turn undo (it costs the turn's time); T1 and S3 say "Marked wrong? Fix it when time's up." |
| 7 | **T1 is cluttered**: up to five tips and six buttons bury "I'm ready" | 4/6 | annoyed | L7: at most one tip at a time; phone tips once per phone, not every turn; "Someone else goes first" |
| 8 | **The tilt rule is only read aloud once**; late joiners and kids who skip S3 miss it | 3/6 | confused | L8: T1 always shows "Tilt down: Got it · Tilt up: Pass" (tap mode: "Tap bottom: Got it · Tap top: Pass") |
| 9 | **A guest can't get in**: no "Join"; adding a player is buried in the menu | 2/6 | stuck | L9: picker card says "One phone, passed round"; a visible "Players (6) ›" on T1 and R1 |
| 10 | **Undo toast vanishes** before slow or busy hands reach it | 4/6 | annoyed | L10: "Score saved · Undo" stays until the next "I'm ready" |
| 11 | **Hard words on S2**: "Non-veg food words", "Sound and buzz"; which pack is picked? | 4/6 | confused | L11: "Include non-veg food (chicken, fish, egg)"; "Sounds" with "So the guesser knows a word counted"; a tick on the chosen pack |
| 12 | **"Skip this turn" beside "Carry on"**: a cousin can skip me; effect not stated | 2/6 | annoyed | L12: Skip asks first and says what counts |
| 13 | **Resume after a long gap** shows the same word 40 minutes later | 1/6 | confused | L13: after any pause, Carry on shows a new word; the hidden word goes back unused |
| 14 | **Score fixing**: anyone can move words, nothing marked; no per-player breakdown; late joiners have fewer turns | 1/6 (uncle) | confused | L14: moved words marked ↺; locked after Save; R1 and G1 show turns played; ties stay shared (WA7, decided) |
| 15 | **A1 resume row** is a wall of small print | 2/6 | annoyed | L15: two lines, the step first |
| 16 | **G2 "Oops, keep playing"**: "Oops" not known | 1/6 | confused | L16: "Back to the game" |
| 17 | Phone **Back** mid-game not shown | 1/6 | confused | covered by L1 (Back pauses) |

Kept, as players praised them: "Not Riya?" escape (renamed), the Undo toasts, "Keep playing" as the safe main button on
every End dialog, the grandparent hint on turn length.

**Not changed (decided):** ties are shared (WA7); the uncle wanted a sudden-death round. It can be a later option if the
owner wants it.
