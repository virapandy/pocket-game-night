# What Am I?: the first family game on the preview (before the families' release)

Play one evening on the preview link with at least 4 people (a child and a grandparent if you can), two or more rounds, at
least one turn hands-free. Afterwards, answer these five in a line or two each. Nothing else is needed; the product owner
turns the answers into changes.

1. **Getting going.** How long from opening the app to the first word on the screen? Did anyone need you to explain how to
   hold the phone or what to do?
2. **Got it and Pass.** Did moving the phone down and up (or the taps, hands-free) mark the right thing? Did a word ever get
   marked by mistake, and was it easy to fix when time was up?
3. **Seeing and hearing.** Could the room read the word from where they sat? Could the guesser tell from the sound (or buzz)
   that a word counted?
4. **Stuck or wrong.** Was there any moment the game didn't do what people expected, or someone tapped and nothing happened?
   Any word nobody knew, or that felt wrong for your family?
5. **Again?** Would this group play it again next time? What one thing would make it better?

If something went badly wrong, a photo of the screen helps more than a description.
